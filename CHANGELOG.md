# Changes

Major updates get a new version. Small fixes and tweaks between versions are not listed.

## v0.1 (October 2026)

First release.

- Four steps: Stockpiles, Criteria, Trials and Compare.
- SMA, dense coarse-graded and dense fine-graded mixes, 4.75 to 37.5 mm NMAS.
- Trial 1 from Bailey inputs, then suggested blends from tested trials, with predicted VMA, air voids and Bailey values.
- Agency requirements: FAA P-401, P-403 and P-404, and UFGS airfield asphalt, SMA and fuel resistant.
- Runs in the browser. Mixes stay in the browser and can be downloaded as files.
